# Design Quality Logic Template

Analyze design sense before listing surface elements.

Required questions:

1. Where does the design sense come from?
2. What are the key design decisions?
3. Which parts show human control rather than AI randomness?
4. How is hierarchy established?
5. Is there a clear layout skeleton?
6. What is restrained?
7. What would reduce the design quality if added?
8. How is the theme translated into visual form?
9. What should be reused: elements or design judgment?
